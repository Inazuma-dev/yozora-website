01 OPEN-SOURCE IOS BROWSER

Yozora,
AI browser.

Yozora is an experimental, open-source AI-powered browser in development for iOS 15.0–16.x.x. It aims to make browsing more intuitive, personal, and intelligent.

03 SIX-MONTH OUTLOOK

A month-by-month build.

A proposed sequence for the next six months. Priorities and timing may change as the project develops.

MONTH 01FOUNDATION

Browser basics

  • URL and search input
  • Navigation, loading, and error states
MONTH 02TABS

Multiple tabs

  • Create, switch, and close tabs
  • Restore the browsing session
MONTH 03LIBRARY

Bookmarks and history

  • Save and revisit pages
  • Keep browsing data on-device
MONTH 04PAGE TOOLS

Ask this page

  • Summarize the current page
  • Prototype questions about its content
MONTH 05AI TOOLS

Search and privacy

  • AI-assisted search flow
  • Make external data sharing clear
MONTH 06BETA

Test and refine

  • Test on iOS 15 and 16 devices
  • Polish, fix, and prepare a beta
